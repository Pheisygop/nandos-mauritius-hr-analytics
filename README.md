# Nando’s Mauritius HR Analytics
A Power BI portfolio project exploring workforce management across six Nando’s Mauritius casas: GBLC, Cascavale, Phoenix, Bagatelle, RTM and Soflaw.

**All employee records, salaries and analytical results are simulated. This is an independent portfolio project, not an official Nando’s report.**

## Report pages
1. **Workforce Overview** — headcount, recruitment, departures and gross pay.
2. **Retention & Movement** — turnover and employee movement.
3. **Attendance & Overtime** — absence, leave and overtime.
4. **Pay & Development** — payroll, training and engagement.
5. **Casa Staffing & Central Support** — role coverage, full-time equivalents, Brand Ambassadors and organisational structure.

## Tools and skills
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query and DAX
- Data modelling and relationships
- Python for simulated data generation
- Workforce KPI definitions and validation

## Staffing model
The planning baseline is approximately 33 employees per casa, including part-time waiters. Each casa has three Brand Ambassadors selected from existing staff.
Central Support represents head office. Its organisational structure is documented separately; central employees are excluded from casa headcount and payroll totals.
